Meet Florence

Where imagination meets thoughtful planning.

I am Florence Botwe Bempong, a creative entrepreneur, Founder and Design Director of DeArk Decor and Home Solutions and Founder and Events Director of Vino Neuvo Events.

Creativity has always been a part of who I am, but so has my love for structure, organisation and the finer details. I believe the magic happens when imagination meets thoughtful planning and purposeful execution.

My journey has been shaped by a blend of education, leadership, professional experience and a lifelong passion for creativity. An alumna of Wesley Girls’ High School, I went on to study Business Administration at Ashesi University College, where I majored in Entrepreneurship. I also hold a Master’s degree in Measurement and Evaluation, further strengthening my analytical, strategic and problem-solving abilities.

Professionally, I have served as a Project Associate with USAID and a Project Manager at Aerodynamics Limited, alongside taking on various leadership responsibilities over the years. These experiences have strengthened my abilities in strategic planning, coordination, problem-solving, stakeholder engagement and delivering results with excellence.

I'm someone who takes both my work and the people I work with seriously. I value integrity, clear communication, attention to detail and genuine relationships. While I thrive on systems and structure, I also believe in flexibility, empathy and understanding that every client, project and vision is unique.

Through DeArk Decor and Home Solutions and Vino Neuvo Events, I have the privilege of expressing two things I deeply love: creating spaces that inspire and curating experiences that bring people together.

At the heart of everything I do is a simple desire: to serve with passion, create with purpose and deliver beyond expectations.

Meet the Founder

Florence Botwe Bempong, Founder and Events Director.

For Florence, event planning is more than a profession. It is a lifelong passion rooted in family, faith, creativity and a genuine love for bringing people together.

The inspiration behind Vino Neuvo

The name Vino Neuvo, meaning New Wine, draws its inspiration from the biblical account of Jesus’ first miracle at the wedding in Cana, recorded in John 2, where He transformed water into wine.

What makes this story particularly meaningful to Florence is not simply the miracle itself, but the extraordinary experience it created. What began as an ordinary wedding celebration became an unforgettable occasion, with the finest wine served when it was least expected.

For Florence, every event presents an opportunity to create something extraordinary, to go beyond what is anticipated and to leave guests with memories that linger long after the celebration has ended.

The story behind DeArk

Meet the Founder. Florence Botwe Bempong, Founder and Design Director.

For Florence, the love for beautiful spaces is a passion rooted in childhood, nurtured by family and shaped by a lifelong appreciation for creativity, comfort and the transformative power of design.

Growing up in a family of creatives, Florence was surrounded by artistic expression in its many forms. Her mother, a naturally gifted creative, explored fashion design, hairdressing, baking and various forms of craftsmanship. One of Florence’s fondest childhood memories is a beautiful floral artwork intricately crafted from beads, framed and displayed in their family living room. Only years later, upon asking her mother about it, did she discover her mother had created it herself.

That lifelong passion led to the establishment of DeArk Decor and Home Solutions, a brand built on creativity, thoughtful design, functionality and a genuine understanding of the emotional connection people have with their spaces. For Florence, interior design is about creating environments that influence how people feel, think, connect and function.

A beautifully designed space should do more than please the eye. It should enrich the experience of living, working and being within it.
